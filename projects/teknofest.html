International Aerospace Competition · TEKNOFEST 2025 · Türkiye

Autonomous Rotary-Wing UAV

A multidisciplinary UAV project developed by Team MACH-X for the TEKNOFEST 2025 Rotary-Wing UAV Competition in Türkiye.

TEAM LEADERSHIP

Team Captain & Safety Pilot — Team MACH-X

I led an eight-member PIEAS team in the TEKNOFEST 2025 Rotary-Wing UAV Competition in Türkiye.

PROJECT SUMMARY

Team MACH-X developed a quadcopter for an autonomous flight and payload-delivery mission at TEKNOFEST 2025. The project combined airframe development, flight control, telemetry, onboard vision, and a payload-release mechanism.

LEADERSHIP

My contribution

As Team Captain and Safety Pilot, I led an eight-member team and contributed to airframe design, avionics integration, onboard image processing, autonomous navigation, and field testing.

SYSTEM DEVELOPMENT

  • Designed the airframe and servo-actuated payload-release mechanism in SolidWorks, using CFD to examine airflow and propeller downwash.
  • Integrated the airframe, propulsion, avionics, and payload subsystems and carried out repeated field trials of autonomous target acquisition and payload delivery.
  • Used a Pixhawk 2.4.8 with ArduPilot for flight control, MAVLink telemetry, and Mission Planner and QGroundControl for ground-station work.
  • Developed custom pymavlink scripts for Raspberry Pi–Pixhawk communication, autonomous navigation, and fail-safe logic.
  • Implemented an OpenCV pipeline on Raspberry Pi 4 for HSV segmentation, morphological filtering, and contour-centroid estimation. Target acquisition triggered the servo-driven payload release.
