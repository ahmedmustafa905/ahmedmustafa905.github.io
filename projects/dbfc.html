Final Design & Lessons Learned

During this competition, I gained practical experience in the aerodynamic and structural design of a fixed-wing aircraft, applying engineering principles to translate theoretical concepts into a functional aircraft configuration. The work involved analyzing the aircraft's aerodynamic performance, including lift, drag, pressure distribution, airflow behavior, and the relationship between thrust, airspeed, and takeoff requirements.

I conducted Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) analysis to investigate airflow around the aircraft and evaluate key aerodynamic characteristics. In parallel, Finite Element Analysis (FEA) was performed to assess structural integrity, stress distribution, deformation, and the ability of the airframe to withstand the expected aerodynamic and operational loads. These analyses provided an engineering basis for refining the aircraft geometry and structural design before fabrication.

The project also strengthened my understanding of aircraft stability and control through the design and analysis of primary control surfaces, including ailerons and elevators. Overall, the competition provided hands-on exposure to the multidisciplinary aircraft design process, integrating aerodynamics, structural analysis, propulsion, stability, control, and engineering validation into a complete fixed-wing aircraft system.
