02 — ABOUT

An engineer built through
cross-domain integration.

I am an Electrical & Electronics Engineering graduate from the Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS).

My undergraduate work took me across embedded electronics, UAVs, autonomous systems, and ROS, alongside digital systems, signal processing, software-defined radio, robotics, and machine learning algorithms — building a foundation that spans hardware, computation, and intelligent decision-making. What began as isolated work on circuits and controllers evolved into a deliberate pursuit of multidisciplinary systems: integrating sensing, computation, communication, and autonomous decision-making into functioning platforms.

Leading student engineering teams in aerospace competitions has been one of the most defining parts of my journey. My strongest international experience came as Team Captain of Team MACH-X, which represented Pakistan as a finalist at TEKNOFEST 2025 in Türkiye, competing in the Rotary-Wing UAV category.
